## Hi, I'm Erica.

ELM Vintage is a small shop of pieces I love. It's where my collection of vintage costume jewelry — built up over years of estate sales, antique malls, and quiet weekend afternoons with my mom — finally finds homes that will love it as much as I do.

Vintage costume jewelry is, to me, the friendliest corner of antiques. It's wearable. It's storied. It survived eighty, a hundred, sometimes a hundred and fifty years to land on a tray in front of me, and I think the right answer to that is to send it back out into the world, into someone else's everyday.

I also make ceramics, on the side, in my studio at home — small things, mostly. Ornaments. Pins. Magnets. The kind of pieces that fit in your hand and on a windowsill. They're a quieter practice than the hunt for vintage, and they've grown alongside it.

Every piece that ships from ELM Vintage goes out with one of those small ceramic things tucked into the package, plus a thank-you card. Nothing big — just a little reminder that there's a person on the other end, and that person is glad it's going home with you.

If you'd like to be the first to see new pieces, [join the list](#) — subscribers get a 24-hour preview before pieces go up on eBay, Etsy, and the public storefront.

— Erica
